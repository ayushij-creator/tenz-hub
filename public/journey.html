Career timeline

Journey

2020

Joins Cloud9

One of the first Counter-Strike pros to move to Valorant, TenZ started his career on Cloud9's first Valorant roster. Source: VLR.gg

JAN 2021

Steps away to stream

He announced he was leaving pro Valorant to focus on content creation. Source: Wikipedia

APR 2021

Loaned to Sentinels

Cloud9 loaned him to Sentinels after a roster suspension, and he played the first stages remotely from Canada. Sentinels bought out his contract on June 2, 2021. Source: Wikipedia

2021

Masters Reykjavík champion

Sentinels swept Fnatic 3–0 in the grand final of the first international Valorant tournament, and TenZ was named grand finals MVP. Source: Wikipedia

MAR 2024

Masters Madrid champion

Sentinels beat Gen.G 3–2 in the grand final, giving TenZ his second Masters title. Source: Wikipedia

SEP 2024

Retires from pro play

On September 14, 2024, he announced his retirement from competitive Valorant after four years of competing, and moved fully into content creation. Source: VLR.gg
